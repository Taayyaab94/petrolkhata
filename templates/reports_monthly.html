"Net Liters Sold" and "Net Revenue" (gross minus that fuel's own returns) are what Cost and Margin are actually computed from - a returned liter didn't stay sold, so it isn't costed or credited as revenue here, only shown separately so the deduction stays visible. Cost per liter is the weighted average of every purchase of that fuel up to the end of this month - a stable, explainable approximation rather than strict per-delivery (FIFO) lot tracking.
