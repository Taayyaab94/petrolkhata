An account's type label is just for organizing/searching - any account can have both credit given to it and credit taken from it (e.g. another pump you sometimes buy from and sometimes sell to). The Debitors/Creditors split above is based purely on each account's current balance, so an account can move between the two as its balance changes. Bank accounts are the pump's own money, not a debitor/creditor relationship, so they only appear under "All".
