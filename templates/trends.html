Profit Over Time

Fuel revenue net of sales returns, minus the cost of the fuel actually sold (net liters sold × weighted average purchase cost, also net of that fuel's returns), plus non-fuel product margin (revenue minus each line's own exact snapshotted cost - no weighted average needed there), minus expenses and salaries - the same definition the Monthly Report uses, so the two always reconcile for the same month. Costing the fuel sold rather than the fuel bought is what stops a tanker delivery from showing as a loss on the day it arrives.
