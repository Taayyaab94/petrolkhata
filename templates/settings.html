Tank Dip Charts

A dip stick measures depth, not volume. Paste the tank's calibration table here - one depth_cm,liters pair per line (commas or tabs both work, so you can paste straight from a spreadsheet) - and the Ledger will collect dips in cm and convert automatically, interpolating between the points you give. Tanks left without a chart keep taking dips in liters directly. Saving replaces that tank's whole chart; saving an empty box clears it.
