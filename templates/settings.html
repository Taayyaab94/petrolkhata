Import Products

Paste a price list here - one product per line, fields separated by commas or tabs (so pasting straight out of a spreadsheet works, same as the dip chart paste below). Column order: name, pack size, purchase rate, retail rate, opening stock, low-stock alert - the last two are optional; when a line omits them, a NEW product gets 0. Use - or leave pack size blank when there isn't one. Category and unit below apply to the WHOLE pasted batch, so paste lubricants together, then filters, separately. Re-pasting the same names later with new rates UPDATES them (keeping full rate history) instead of creating duplicates - matching is by name, case-insensitive. Nothing is imported if any line has an error.

For a product that ALREADY EXISTS, an import updates only its rates, pack size, category and unit - plus the low-stock alert, but only on a line that actually supplies one; leaving that 6th column blank keeps whatever threshold is already set (e.g. from the single-row form above) instead of resetting it to 0. Opening stock for an existing product is never touched by an import - to adjust stock, record a purchase or edit the product directly.

Example - two lines, tab or comma separated:
HELIX H3, 3 L, 3246, 3590, 40, 10
OIL FILTER - COROLLA, -, 350, 500

A price is a historical fact with a date, not one current number - each entry is billed at whichever price was in effect on its own date. So when backfilling older records, set that period's price here first (e.g. "Rs 402.10 from 1 May"), then enter those days. Setting a past price does not disturb today's price; the most recent one still applies now.

If a date has no price recorded on or before it, entries for that date fall back to today's price - which is why the Ledger warns you when that's the case. Each entry stores its own price at the moment it's saved, so correcting a price later will not repair entries already made; use the re-pricing tool below for those.

Re-price Past Entries Owner

Corrected a price after already entering some days? Those entries kept the price they were saved at - this recalculates them against the corrected price history, for a whole date range at once, instead of re-saving each day on the Ledger by hand.

You'll see exactly what would change - every entry's old and new amount, side by side - and nothing is written until you confirm on that screen. Litres are never altered, only the price and the money that follows from it. Credit entries recorded as a deliberate discount ("By Amount") are left alone.
