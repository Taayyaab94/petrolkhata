Net Cash Flow = sales - credit given + receipts - expenses - cash-paid purchases - supplier payments - salaries paid - cash-refunded sales returns + cash-method non-fuel sales - cash-paid product purchases + cash-method other income. Purchases made on supplier credit aren't subtracted at the time of purchase since that cash hasn't left yet - it's counted when the supplier is actually paid; a sales return (or non-fuel sale, or product purchase, or other income) settled via a bank or a customer's/supplier's account is excluded here the same way, since it doesn't move cash-in-hand (see that bank's balance, or the account's balance, instead). For a period profit view (revenue against the cost of fuel and products actually sold), see the Monthly Report.
